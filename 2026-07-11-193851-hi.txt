  app/api/category-sizes/route.ts:28,80-86 — PAGE_SIZE = 200, then fetches all

  self-inflicted load spike / timeout risk. (Masked today by C1, but will
  surface the moment C1 is fixed.) Add a concurrency cap or use a single
  aggregation query instead of paginating full product sets.
